Try the boss without playing a course. The same forward perspective opens into a wider arena.

Absorb → change sign → counter. Same-color ±4 blobs add to your army. Opposite colors cancel. Approach with the opposite sign to cancel its body continuously. Both become smaller together. Retreat before your army reaches zero.

There are two active operation spots: red makes a negative army, blue makes a positive army. Each has an addition or multiplication operation with an exact result preview. After use, that color reappears at a different spot.

Dr. Fin starts at +100. Watch the warning, then avoid or use the ×(−1) sign wave. He changes sign and releases four groups of 4. Those groups retain their sign even when Dr. Fin changes. Opposite signs reduce his health; matching signs heal him. A zero army is eliminated.

Two operation circles are active: red gives a negative result and blue a positive result. Each relocates after use, with a brief recharge.
